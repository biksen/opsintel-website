Our First Product

Meet Fulcrum

Fulcrum Live Product
Governed AI for software delivery

From a red pipeline to a fix your team can trust.

Fulcrum is an agentic teammate for the delivery pipeline. When a build fails or a deploy breaks, it reads the logs, pinpoints the root cause, and drafts the smallest fix as a pull request that runs through your normal review — a human still approves the merge. Every step is policy-checked, budget-bounded, and written to a tamper-evident record, so the automation is auditable, never taken on faith.
