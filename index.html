Meet Fulcrum

Fulcrum
Governed AI for software delivery

From a red pipeline to a fix your team can trust.

Fulcrum is an agentic teammate for the delivery pipeline. Every step is policy-checked, budget-bounded, and written to a tamper-evident record — so the automation is auditable, never taken on faith.

How it works
  1. 1
    A build or deploy fails An event arrives from your pipeline.
  2. 2
    Fulcrum diagnoses the root cause It reads the logs and forms a hypothesis.
  3. 3
    It drafts the smallest fix Opens a pull request and runs your CI.
  4. 4
    Your team approves and merges A human owns the merge — always.
Root-cause intelligence Cut MTTR from hours to minutes. Fulcrum pinpoints the root cause of a failed build or deploy — so engineers fix, not hunt.
Institutional memory Never solves the same failure twice. Fulcrum recognises a recurring failure and applies the fix that already worked — your whole incident history, put to work automatically.
Governed fixes, human-approved AI that proposes, never disposes. Every fix ships as a pull request through your review — your policies and your people decide what merges.
Security & compliance Audit-ready by design. SSO, role-based access, and vaulted secrets — plus a tamper-evident record that yields SOC 2 and EU AI Act evidence in one click.
Measured value & reliability Numbers for the board. DORA metrics, step-latency SLOs, and eval-gated changes make reliability provable — while per-run budget caps and an instant kill-switch keep AI spend forecastable and the ROI one you can show finance.
Plug-and-play, vendor-neutral Value in weeks, not a re-platforming. Connects to the CI/CD, cloud, and source tools you already run — GitHub, GitLab, Azure, Jenkins, AWS. No rip-and-replace, no lock-in.

Built for velocity. Designed for control. Ship faster with AI that follows your rules.
