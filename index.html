FulcrumAgentic SDLC

Diagnose. Fix. Prove it — without leaving the guardrails.

Fulcrum reads the failing logs, finds the root cause, drafts the smallest change, and runs it through the normal lifecycle. A human still merges; everything is logged where it can be audited.

  • Governed at every gate — policy checks and human approval before any change merges.
  • Provable, not just logged — a hash-chained audit trail and one-click evidence for SOC 2 & EU AI Act reviews.
  • FinOps guardrails — per-run, daily & monthly spend caps with an instant kill-switch.
  • Reliability you can measure — DORA metrics, deploy diagnosis, and anomaly-aware traces.
